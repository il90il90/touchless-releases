How it works

From install to "just do it" in three steps

No root, no PC and no developer skills needed. Touchless runs quietly in the background and keeps a secure line open to your AI.

Safety first

Powerful, but never reckless.

Giving AI access to your phone needs real guardrails. We built them in from the start.

You approve the risky stuff

Sending messages, calling, purchasing or deleting always asks for confirmation first. Read-only actions run automatically.

Verified, not assumed

Every action is checked against the real screen. Uncertain actions are never retried blindly.

Encrypted secrets

API keys are stored with AES-256 in the Android Keystore. The relay keeps only a hash of your token, and passwords are never read.

You own the connection

Use a per-device revocable token and an optional IP allow-list. You can also point Touchless at your own relay or a local LLM.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

What exactly is Touchless?

Touchless is an Android app that gives AI assistants such as Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini, Grok or a local LLM safe, structured control of your phone through the Model Context Protocol (MCP). Your AI can see the screen, operate apps, send messages, manage files and run automations for you.

Which AI assistants does it work with?

Any client that supports MCP over Streamable HTTP, including Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini, Grok and LM Studio. For on-device auto-replies you can bring your own API key for OpenAI, Anthropic, Google Gemini, xAI, LM Studio or any OpenAI-compatible service.

Do I need root or a computer?

No. Touchless works with standard Android permissions and a built-in cloud relay, with zero setup. Shizuku, root or Sui are optional and add extra privileged actions. Advanced users can also point the app at their own relay server.

Is it safe to let an AI control my phone?

Touchless is built around safety. Sending, calling, buying and deleting always need your confirmation. Every action is checked on the real screen, secrets are encrypted in the Android Keystore, one-time codes are masked, and password fields are never read. You can revoke your connection token at any time.

Why isn't it on Google Play?

Touchless needs deep Accessibility and automation capabilities that the Play Store limits. We publish signed APKs with SHA-256 checksums through our official release channel, and the app updates itself in place.

How does licensing work?

Every plan includes every feature. A key activates one device, and the app keeps working offline for up to 24 hours between license checks. The Lifetime plan never expires.

Does it work on Android TV?

Yes, in beta. Touchless includes a TV-optimized interface with remote-friendly navigation. Setup runs from Touchless on your phone over Wireless debugging: scan the QR on the TV, type the 6-digit pairing code, and the phone turns everything on, with no Termux or computer. Then your AI can control your Android TV or Google TV too.
